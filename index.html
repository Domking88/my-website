For The Person I Love

I know I was wrong. I know there were times na nasaktan kita, disappointed you, and made you feel na parang hindi ko pinahalagahan yung feelings mo.

And I'm sorry. Hindi ko gustong sabihin yung "sorry" para lang matapos yung problema. I'm saying sorry because I finally understand that my actions had consequences.

Nag-promise ako sayo na hindi kita iiwan. And I know... nasira ko yung promise na yun. Hindi ko na mababago yung nangyari, pero kaya kong aminin na nagkamali ako.

I still love you. Sobra. And if you ask me kung sino yung gusto kong piliin, ikaw pa rin yung taong nasa puso ko.

Hindi ko masasabi kung ano ang magiging ending ng story natin. Pero kung bibigyan man tayo ng pagkakataon na magsimula ulit, I don't want to make promises just with words. I want to prove it through my actions.

I want to learn how to love you better. Not perfectly... but genuinely.

✦ ONE LAST SECRET ✦

May isa pa akong gustong sabihin sa'yo...

Kung magkakaroon man tayo ng multiverse... sana sa ibang universe, maging masaya sila.

Sana doon, mas marunong na akong magmahal. Sana hindi ko na siya nasaktan. Sana mas marami silang memories na maganda, mas maraming tawanan, at mas kaunting dahilan para umiyak.

Sana sa universe na yun, kapag may problema, hindi kami susuko agad. Sana matuto kaming makinig sa isa't isa. Sana matuto kaming pahalagahan yung meron kami.

And if somehow, pareho pa rin tayo sa kahit anong universe... sana piliin pa rin namin ang isa't isa. Not because we have to... but because we genuinely want to.

Maybe in another universe, we got everything right. But in this universe... I still hope that someday, we can find a reason to smile again. ❤️
